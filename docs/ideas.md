# Ideas

Design ideas that are not rules (yet). Not shown in the game.

## Time

### Free cards with a catch

A card can cost 0 time and still carry a drawback or a punishment. For example, a spell that costs 0 but moves the timer 10 toward your opponent.

### Cards for when you are behind

Cards that can only be played while you are behind on the timer, to help a player catch up.

### Cards for when you are far ahead

Cards that can only be played if you are more than X ahead on the timer.

### Cards that scale with the timer

Cards whose effect depends on the timer, for example more damage the further ahead you are, to punish overextending.

### Cards that cost X

Cards with a time cost of X, where you choose how much time to pour into them when you play them, and the more you pour in, the stronger the effect.

## Winning and fighting

### How to attack?

How do creatures attack, and what do they attack?

### How to win?

What wins the game, besides the other player running out of cards?

### Running out of cards

If you have no cards in your deck and have to draw, you lose. (This is already how the game works.)

## Playing cards

### Mulligan

A mulligan where you pay the time cost of every card you take back.

### Activating abilities without spamming

How can cards activate their abilities without players spamming them?

### A stack?

Is there a stack, as in Magic, where cards and abilities wait to resolve and can be answered?

### Trap cards

Cards played face down first, which you can turn up and activate whenever you want.

## Permanents

### Equipment and auras

Permanents attached to other permanents, saying what they can attach to. Two kinds:

- Equipment: something with an ability to attach (and move) to a permanent.
- Auras: attached to a permanent, and die whenever the permanent they are attached to dies.

### Permanents without attack or health

Permanents that have neither attack nor health.

## Zones

### Exile?

Is there an exile zone? Or are cards put into the graveyard face down instead?
